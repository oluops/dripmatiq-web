I used to have a "favorite sweater." Singular. One piece I reached for when I didn't want to think.

It was a cream merino cardigan. $180. Worn maybe 40 times over three years. Cost per wear: $4.50. Not bad.

But here's the thing — I owned six other sweaters that served the exact same occasions. A navy cashmere. A charcoal wool blend. A rust chunky knit. Two more merinos in olive and gray. All bought because "I need a sweater for work." All worn 2-5 times each. Cost per wear: $30-80.

The cream one won because it was visible. Front of the drawer. Top of the mental index. The others? Buried. Forgotten. Invisible.

## The Query That Changed Everything

Tuesday morning. 68°F, rain, commute + office + drinks after.

Old me: check three weather apps. Stand in closet for ten minutes. Trench? Too heavy if rain stops. Blazer? Not waterproof. Cardigan? Useless in rain. Ten minutes of anxiety.

New me: open app. Today's look loads automatically.

Trench coat (waterproof, mild, commute). Merino base layer (mild, work, all-day, loafers). Straight-leg jeans (mild, casual, all-day, loafers or boots). Ankle boots (waterproof, commute, walking).

Virtual try-on shows the fit. The proportion. The tuck. The drape. I see it on *me* before I put anything on.

Thirty seconds. Dressed. Zero trying on three things. Zero regret.
